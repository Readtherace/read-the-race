# Observations

An append-only log of unusual factors noticed in the record, kept under
the Overfitting rule in each framework: *"Record unusual factors under
observation. Promote them into the framework only after repeated,
recorded evidence."* Entries here are notes only — they carry no
predictive weight, do not change any locked rule, and are never used to
justify a selection or a confidence grade in any entry, past or future.

Distinct from `CORRECTIONS.md` (documentation issues in a committed
entry) and `RETRACTIONS.md` (entries removed from the record). Nothing
here alters an entry file, `results.csv` row, or `manifest.json` entry
beyond the result/starting-price fields those are already allowed to
carry once known.
